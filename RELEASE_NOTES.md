## What's Changed

### Improvements
- Zombies spread across the Zone instead of piling up at a few lairs: their random patrols now also head for resources and open territory. This is in the Balanced and All Out War presets, so apply the preset again on an existing save

### Bug Fixes
- Fix random patrols staying forever at lairs, resources and open territory. Idle random patrols on any smart now move on to a new target from time to time; at night they stay put unless the smart's night activity roll lets them go
